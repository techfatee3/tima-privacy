Tima Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Short version: Tima does not collect, send or share your personal data. Everything you write stays on your phone.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Tima has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own.

Information stored on your device

To work, Tima saves the following only on your phone: the name you enter, your daily mood and energy check-ins, your journal entries, and your habits and their completion history. This information is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Third parties, ads and analytics

Tima contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not share data with anyone.

Permissions

Tima does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Deleting your data

Go to the You tab and tap Erase all my data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything Tima has stored.

Children

Tima is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Tima is a self-care companion and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are in crisis or feel unsafe, please contact your local emergency or crisis service.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
